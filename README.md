# 🎵 MirrorVibes (SWiftie Edition)

> *Your mood. Your music.*

**MirrorVibes** is a mood-based music recommendation web app that reflects how you feel through music — powered by the iTunes API.

🔗 **Live Demo:** [mirrorvibes.onrender.com](https://mirrorvibes.onrender.com/)

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎧 Mood-Based Playlist Recommender
Pick your current mood and instantly get a curated playlist of songs that match your vibe. Choose from moods like *happy, sad, chill, hype, romantic, nostalgic* and more.

### 🌟 MirrorVibes: Swiftie Edition
A Taylor Swift fan? This edition recommends Taylor Swift songs and albums based on your mood. Feeling melancholic? Get *Evermore*. Feeling euphoric? Say hello to *1989*. Every mood has its era.

### 🌈 Dynamic Background
The background of the app dynamically shifts to match Taylor Swift's album aesthetic based on your selected mood — from the golden warmth of *Fearless* to the dark mystique of *reputation*.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Backend:** Node.js, Express.js
- **Frontend:** EJS, CSS
- **API:** iTunes Search API
